Metric Descriptions

MSE (Mean Squared Error)

Measures the average squared difference between pixel values. Lower values indicate higher similarity.

MSE = (1/N) × Σ(Original - Transformed)²
PSNR (Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio)

Measures image quality in decibels. Higher values indicate better quality. Typically 30-50 dB for good quality.

PSNR = 10 × log₁₀(MAX² / MSE)
SSIM (Structural Similarity Index)

Measures structural similarity considering luminance, contrast, and structure. Range [0, 1], closer to 1 is better.

SSIM = f(luminance, contrast, structure)
