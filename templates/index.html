DSP Face Lab

A powerful facial image processing platform built with computer vision and digital signal processing. Warp expressions, simulate aging, and analyze transformations with real-time frequency domain insights.

Get Started → Explore Features

Core Capabilities

End-to-end facial transformation and analysis pipeline

📸

Upload & Detect

Upload facial images, auto-detect face regions, and extract 468+ facial landmarks with precision.

🔮

Expression Warp

Enhance smiles, raise eyebrows, widen lips, and slim faces using triangle-based geometric deformation.

⏳

Age Simulation

Simulate aging or rejuvenation using frequency-domain filtering — wrinkle enhancement and skin smoothing.

📊

Analyze & Evaluate

Fourier spectrum analysis, spectral energy metrics, and quality evaluation with MSE, PSNR & SSIM.
