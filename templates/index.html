Metric Reference

MSE

Mean Squared Error — average pixel difference squared. Lower = better.

MSE = &frac1N; Σ(O − T)²

PSNR

Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio in dB. Higher = better. >30 dB is good.

PSNR = 10 log₁₀(MAX² / MSE)

SSIM

Structural Similarity. Range [0,1]. Closer to 1 = better.

SSIM = f(lum, contrast, structure)
